Privacy Policy

At Quantasec, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in a secure and transparent manner. This policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal data in compliance with applicable UAE regulations, including SIRA standards.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • IP address and device/browser information when you access our website
  • Contact details (e.g. name, email, phone number) submitted through forms
  • Service usage data for maintenance and system performance analysis
  • Security footage and access logs in controlled environments (for clients)

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain our security and ELV solutions
  • To comply with SIRA and local security regulations
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide technical support
  • To improve our website and system integrations
  • To analyze security events and provide proactive monitoring (where applicable)

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Enhance your browsing experience
  • Understand how you interact with our site
  • Collect anonymous usage statistics

You may accept or decline cookies via the cookie consent popup or adjust your browser settings at any time.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell or trade your personal information. Data may be shared with third-party vendors and subcontractors only for:

  • Service delivery and maintenance contracts
  • Compliance with legal obligations and government audits
  • Regulatory reporting to authorities such as SIRA

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion (subject to legal or regulatory requirements)
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@quantasec.ae.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard measures to safeguard your data including encryption, secure access controls, and compliance with UAE cyber and physical security regulations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
