QuantaSec offers robust gate barrier and turnstile solutions that ensure secure and efficient management of both vehicle and pedestrian entry points. Our systems include automatic boom barriers, swing and sliding gates, and high-speed turnstiles, all designed for smooth flow and stringent control.

Integrated with advanced technologies like license plate recognition (LPR), RFID, and QR-based access, our solutions offer real-time monitoring and seamless integration with visitor management and access control platforms. Whether for commercial complexes, residential communities, or industrial facilities, our systems are built for reliability and ease of use.

Service Overview

Our entry control systems are engineered for high-volume usage with fast response times, intelligent automation, and centralized access control. From underground parking garages to high-security building entrances, we customize solutions to fit every requirement.

  • Automated boom barriers and turnstiles for controlled access.
  • Integrated ANPR and RFID systems for seamless vehicle entry.
  • Real-time tracking and audit trails for compliance and safety.
